Senior Data Analyst — Pricing & Revenue
Company: Maple Commerce (fictional example)
Location: Toronto, ON (Hybrid)

About the role
Maple Commerce is looking for a Senior Data Analyst to join our Pricing & Revenue team. You will own the analytics behind pricing decisions, design and analyze experiments, and build self-serve dashboards for business partners.

Responsibilities
- Design, run, and analyze A/B tests for pricing and promotions.
- Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau or Looker for pricing, revenue, and margin KPIs.
- Write production-quality SQL and Python to build reliable data pipelines.
- Partner with pricing managers and finance to turn analysis into recommendations.
- Mentor junior analysts and raise the bar on analytics best practices.

Requirements
- 4+ years of experience in data analytics, ideally in retail or e-commerce.
- Advanced SQL and Python (pandas).
- Experience with experimentation and statistical methods (A/B testing, regression).
- Experience with price elasticity or revenue management is a strong plus.
- Experience with dbt and Snowflake is a plus.
- Excellent communication skills with non-technical stakeholders.
